How the engine builds PD curves

A Z-score for each year shifts the TTC matrix into that year's PiT matrix. Multiplying the yearly matrices gives cumulative default curves, and differencing them gives the yearly PDs that ECL uses.

Part-year final bucket

When the last bucket is a fraction d of a year, the curve still holds its whole year. The ECL step takes the part the maturity covers, pro rata (owner decision of 18 October 2026, A7). Until then R6 built a part-year row per grade with the constant hazard of PD-06.

m_part = d · m[n+1]
