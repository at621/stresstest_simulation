This is the base of the solution: a working static stress engine for the draft EBA 2027 method on a synthetic book. The draft readings are selectable because the draft can be read more than one way. The preparation analyses are separate diagnostics: their outputs do not replace core losses, and they run on supplied, illustrative inputs. The workbook and exports are not an official EBA template. The requirement counts come from the generated traceability matrix for requirements version 1.3. Compared with the commit before the merge, tested rose from 66 to 68, built fell from 27 to 25, and unmet requirements with no decision fell from 9 to 8 as SCN-03 gained a recorded decision.
