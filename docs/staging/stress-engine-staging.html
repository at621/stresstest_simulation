How staging works in the stress engine

Stage follows the rating grade. A performing grade slice is S2 when its lifetime PD is at least k times the lifetime PD at the facility's origination grade, and S3 when it reaches default grade D. R3 checks this rule against the reported opening stage. R7 applies it to every grade slice in each projection year, 2027 to 2029. Later steps only read the result. Since the owner's decision of 18 October 2026 a performing facility is also S2 when it is 30 or more days past due, on the watchlist or forborne, whatever its grade, and the flag holds it in S2 until it defaults (MIG-05, A30). R3 then checks "grade rule or trigger" against the reported stage.

Triggers in use
  • S2Lifetime PD of the current grade ≥ k × lifetime PD at origination, with k from 2.0 to 3.0 by segment
  • S3Grade migrates to D in the year's matrix
  • S230+ days past due, watchlist or forbearance, any one, held until default (since 18 October 2026)

TransitionsEvery stage move is a grade move

The engine has no separate stage model. Each year the scenario's point-in-time matrix M(y) moves exposure between grades. A slice's stage then follows from where its grade sits against the facility's SICR line, or from reaching D. Three moves are possible, and S3 is absorbing.

WorkflowWhere staging happens in the run

Stage is decided in two places. R3 checks the reported end-2026 stage and fixes each facility's SICR line. R7 sets the stage of every grade slice in each projection year. Every step after R7 reads that stage and never recomputes it. R1 and R11 have no stage logic.

  1. O3

    Generate the synthetic book foundation/synth/

    builds consistent inputs

    Gives each facility an origination grade and a current grade, chosen so that the SICR rule returns its target opening stage (SYN-PAR-08, design D3). It also writes dpd, watchlist and forborne, with their observation date. No S1 facility carries a trigger. Since 18 October 2026, 25% of each segment's S2 facilities are S2 by a trigger alone, with a grade above the SICR line (A30): 331 in input set d62d0386f2c5256e, of which 250 have no S2 grade at all.

    foundation/synth/generate.py · _facility_table, _flag_only, _ensure_trigger

  2. R2

    Load inputs ingest/

    loads stage fields

    Reads ifrs_stage, current_grade, origination_grade, the obligor's regulatory_default, and the three qualitative flags with credit_status_date. Only schema and type checks happen here.

    src/stress/contracts/inputs.py · FACILITIES

  3. R3

    Validate and route validate/

    checks opening stage

    The reported stage is kept, never overwritten. A facility that fails any check below is rejected with its commitment and opening provision, and the run continues.

    1. Stage is 1, 2 or 3. INVALID_STAGE
    2. S3 if and only if the current grade is D. STAGE_GRADE_CONFLICT
    3. The obligor's regulatory default flag agrees with S3 (DAT-04). DEFAULT_STAGE_CONFLICT
    4. POCI is outside v1. POCI_NOT_IN_V1
    5. DPD, watchlist and forbearance are present, never read as "no trigger", and observed no later than the snapshot. MISSING_FLAG INVALID_CREDIT_STATUS_DATE
    6. For each performing facility, compute the SICR line: the first grade, going down the scale, whose lifetime TTC PD over the residual maturity at end-2026 is at least k times that of the origination grade. The facility is S2 when its grade is at or past the line or a switched-on trigger holds; that stage must equal the reported stage. STAGE_GRADE_CONFLICT
    7. Route S3 to the original S3 cohort and S1 and S2 to the performing route. The SICR line is stored on the portfolio as sicr_rank, the trigger as qualitative_trigger.

    Current run: 10,000 accepted, 0 rejected. 9,669 performing, 331 original S3. Every accepted grade explains its stage.

    src/stress/validate/__init__.py · validate_and_route, _check_triggers · src/stress/validate/sicr.py · sicr_rank, qualitative_trigger

  4. R4–R6

    Scenarios, cash flows, parameters scenario/ cashflows/ params/

    feeds grade moves

    R4 turns the scenario into a Z-score per segment, country and year. R6 shifts the TTC matrix with Z into the point-in-time matrix M(y). This is the only route by which the economy reaches stage: it moves grades, never the SICR line. R5 replaces a matured facility like for like, with the same grades and therefore the same stage and line (EXP-05, A6).

  5. R7

    Project the year projection/

    sets stageloops 2027 → 2029, per scenario

    Closing grade slices = opening slices × M(y). Each performing slice then takes its stage from its grade and the facility's stored line:

    S3
    grade = D
    S2
    grade rank ≥ sicr_rank
    S1
    otherwise

    Exposure that reaches D becomes a new S3 cohort and keeps its entry year and origin stage (MIG-03). S3 slices go around the matrix. Stage flows are the sums of matrix cells on each side of the line, so S1↔S2 shares are outputs, not inputs, and default volumes come from one matrix only (MIG-04).

  6. R8

    Year-end provisions ecl/

    reads stage

    S1 slices get 12-month ECL (bucket y+1). S2 slices get lifetime ECL to maturity. Original S3 gets the larger of GCA × LGD and its prior allowance; a new S3 cohort gets its flow × the LGD of its default year. Stage is taken from R7 and not recomputed.

    src/stress/ecl/__init__.py:225–260

  7. R9–R10

    Stock to flow, aggregate and report accounting/ report/

    reads stage

    R9 checks every S3 slice from year to year and blocks the run if one disappears (a cure) or releases provision. R10 builds the stage migration report and reconciles the flows to the closing stage stocks for each scenario, year and portfolio.

R3 and R7The SICR rule and its threshold

The threshold for relative deterioration is k times the lifetime PD at the facility's origination grade. k is set per segment in parameter_set.yaml as sicr_k: 2.0 for consumer credit, 2.5 for mortgages, SME, commercial real estate and corporates, and 3.0 for general government and credit institutions. There is no absolute PD level. In grade terms the threshold is two notches below the origination grade for most facilities.

Stage of a grade slice

S3
if grade = D
S2
if PDlife(g, T) ≥ k × PDlife(g0, T)
S1
otherwise
  • g0 the facility's origination grade.
  • T the residual maturity at 31 Dec 2026, used for both PDs and never updated (A6).
  • PDlife from powers of the segment's TTC matrix; a part year uses constant hazard (A7).
  • k per segment, a test convention rather than an EBA threshold (MIG-05).

What follows from it

  • The line is fixed per facility. Only rating moves change stage.
  • The line uses TTC PDs. A worse scenario raises S2 by pushing grades down, not by moving the line.
  • The rule is symmetric. A slice whose grade moves back above the line is S1 in that same year.
  • Stage belongs to a grade slice, not to a facility. After one projection year most facilities are spread over several grades and can be partly S1 and partly S2.
  • D never cures.

ObservationsObservations and open points

  1. 1

    Qualitative triggers are in use since 18 October 2026

    Until 18 October 2026, MIG-07 and design D3 left out 30+ DPD, watchlist and forbearance for the synthetic books. MIG-05 and PD-06 expect a bank's SICR to use them and to keep them separate. The data does carry them, and O3 built them to agree with the opening stage: 432 S2 facilities are 30–89 days past due, 509 are on the watchlist and 143 are forborne, and no S1 facility has any of the three. In the v1 data leaving them out changed nothing at end-2026.

    Since the owner's decision of 18 October 2026 all three are in use (MIG-05, A30). A flagged facility is S2 in every performing grade until it defaults, because the flags are not projected and are held at their opening value (option (a)). On input set d62d0386f2c5256e, 960 performing facilities carry a trigger. Against the v1 run, S2 GCA is €32m / €59m / €82m higher in baseline 2027 / 2028 / 2029 (€9m / €15m / €25m in the adverse), and provisions are €0.5–0.6m higher in the baseline and €0.3m in the adverse. MIG-07's last sentence and design D3 said the triggers were not used; the requirement text is the owner's to update (C11).

  2. 2

    930 facilities can never reach S2

    For 930 performing facilities (€417.7m GCA, 3.9% of performing GCA, all S1 and all originated at G6 or worse), even G10's lifetime PD is below k times the lifetime PD at origination. The relative test cannot fire, so they can only move from S1 straight to S3. 582 of them are P01 mortgages with a median 24 years to maturity, where lifetime PDs of all grades come close together. No absolute PD level catches them either. Since 18 October 2026 the qualitative triggers give such facilities a route to S2: in input set d62d0386f2c5256e, 250 flag-only S2 facilities (€184.6m) have no S2 grade. The 930 above stay S1, because §5.2 fixes the stage counts and a flag on a reported S1 facility would contradict its stage.

  3. 3

    SICR horizon awaits the owner

    Both lifetime PDs use the residual maturity at end-2026 and keep it for the whole projection (assumption A6). This keeps the line fixed as MIG-07 asks, but conflict C6 notes the tension with PD-06's remaining-life wording. The owner should confirm.

  4. 4

    k values are proposed, and AT-48 has no oracle numbers

    k comes from parameter set SYN-PS-0.1-proposed, status PROPOSED. AT-48 proves the rule without expected numbers; its stage per grade stays OPEN until approved (A10).
